VSC Pitch Lab — local proof of concept, 2026-11-07

The DSP modules dsp.mjs and streaming-dsp.mjs reconstruct/adapt the
SoundTouch 1.7.0 integer pitch-shifting path, based on analysis of VSC 2.2
and the author's corresponding source archive.

https://www.surina.net/soundtouch/
Reference source: https://www.surina.net/soundtouch/soundtouch-1.7.0.tar.gz
The adapted DSP modules are provided under LGPL-2.1-or-later.
The complete DSP source is included in these modules; see LICENSE.txt.

This PoC includes offline and stateful mono streaming JavaScript paths. It is not the
original VSC source or a native/MMX/bit-exact emulation. Tail flushing,
Float32/Int16 conversion, wide accumulation and safe output-length handling
are documented in IMPLEMENTATION.md and DSP-API.md.
